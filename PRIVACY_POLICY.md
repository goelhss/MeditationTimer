# Meditation Timer Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026. The canonical public policy is published at
<https://goelhss.github.io/MeditationTimer/privacy/>.

Meditation Timer stores timer/reminder settings, meditation logs, and resolutions
locally. Optional Google backup uses only Drive's private application-data scope,
keeps one replacement backup capped at 1 MB, and provides restore, delete, and
disconnect controls. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell
personal information. See the public policy above for complete details.
