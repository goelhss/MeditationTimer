Meditation Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Meditation Timer stores the information you enter—timer and reminder settings, meditation logs, and resolutions—on your Android device. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal information.

Google Drive backup

If you explicitly connect a Google Account, the app requests only the Google Drive application-data scope. It writes one private, hidden app-data backup containing your logs, resolutions, timer settings, and reminder settings. The backup is capped at 1 MB and replaces the previous backup instead of accumulating versions. The developer does not operate a server that receives this backup.

You can restore the backup, delete it from Google Drive, or disconnect Google access in the app. Deleting the cloud backup also disables automatic backup so it is not silently recreated. Google processes account authorization and Drive storage under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Other backup and sharing choices

Android may include the same app data in an encrypted device backup or device-to-device transfer when supported by your device. You may also export a readable JSON backup or share meditation logs as a text file to a destination you choose. Files you export are controlled by you and may be readable by anyone who can access that destination.

Permissions

The app uses notifications, alarms, foreground execution, wake lock, vibration, boot events, and internet access to run timers and reminders reliably and to support the optional Google Drive connection. It does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

Retention and deletion

Local entries remain until you delete them or clear the app's data. The single Google Drive app-data backup remains until you replace or delete it, disconnecting alone does not delete it. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them.
